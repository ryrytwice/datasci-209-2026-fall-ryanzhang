The Elite Anatomy of FIFA 20

An interactive visual journey examining performance, age, market value, and positions among top-tier global football players.

1. The Peak Performers: Global player ratings strictly follow a right-skewed distribution, where only a tiny fraction of elite stars surpass an overall rating of 88.

2. Age vs. Quality: Peak performance clusters tightly between ages 26 and 31, while younger prodigies and veteran outliers remain rare exceptions.

3. Market Value Exponentiality: Player valuation scales exponentially with overall rating, creating an immense market premium for top-tier talent.

4. Positional Spread: Midfielders and Forwards dominate top rating tiers, whereas Goalkeepers and Defenders exhibit distinct valuation and rating dynamics.

5. The Full Picture: Hover over any player to inspect their complete profile, market valuation, and attribute distributions.
